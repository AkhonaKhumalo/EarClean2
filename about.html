About Ear Clean

Calm, expert ear and hearing care, delivered online.

Our mission

To make professional audiology advice quick, affordable and easy to reach, so no ear, hearing or balance concern is left waiting.

Our vision

A South Africa where everyone can get trusted hearing guidance in their own language, wherever they live.

Who we help

Adults, parents and caregivers who want clear answers about hearing and ear health.

  • People with muffled hearing, ringing or ear discomfort
  • Parents worried about a child's hearing or speech
  • Anyone with dizziness or balance concerns
  • Hearing aid users and people considering them
  • Clients in rural areas or with limited mobility

Why virtual audiology works

Many ear and hearing concerns start with a conversation: your symptoms, your history and your daily challenges. A focused virtual consultation can give you clear guidance, help you decide whether you need in-person care, and provide a referral letter, all without leaving home. It is not a replacement for a physical examination when one is needed, and we will tell you honestly when that is the case.
