What we can help with

Any ear, hearing or balance concern is welcome.

  • Ear Wax / Blockage

    Muffled hearing, a full feeling or discomfort. We guide safe care and what to avoid.

  • Hearing Loss

    Struggling in noise or turning the TV up? Understand your options and next steps.

  • Tinnitus / Ringing

    Ringing, buzzing or hissing. Learn what may be causing it and how to manage it.

  • Dizziness / Balance

    Spinning or unsteadiness. We help work out when to see a specialist.

  • Ear Pain / Infection Advice

    Guidance on pain, discharge or itching, and when to see a doctor urgently.

  • Hearing Aid Advice

    Thinking of hearing aids, or struggling with yours? Get honest, practical advice.

  • Children's Hearing Concerns

    Speech delays, ear infections or school worries. Gentle guidance for parents.

  • Ear Care Education

    Safe ear care habits, noise protection and healthy hearing for the whole family.
